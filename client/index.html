How to play
  • Town (Villagers, Doctor, Detective) wins when every Mafia member is dead.
  • Mafia win when they equal or outnumber everyone else.
  • Night: the Mafia pick a victim, the Doctor protects someone (not the same player twice in a row), the Detective learns whether someone is Mafia.
  • Day: discuss, then vote to execute someone — or skip. Ties execute nobody.
  • The dead become observers: they see everything but can only talk to other dead players.
